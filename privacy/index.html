Privacy Policy for Inklight

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Inklight is a reading practice app for adults learning English. We built it so your practice stays private.

The short version

  • We do not collect any information about you.
  • You don't need an account. You never sign in.
  • Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.
  • There are no ads and no tracking.
  • The app downloads new reading texts from the internet. It sends nothing about you when it does.

What the app keeps, and where

The app saves your practice on your phone only:

  • your first name, if you type one
  • your photo, if you choose one from your photos
  • your help language and your reading level
  • the texts you read, your answers and what you write
  • the words you keep, and the words and sentences you save from your day
  • your settings (text size, colors, read-aloud voice and speed)

This information never leaves your phone. We can't see it, and no one else can either.

New texts and languages

From time to time, the app downloads new reading texts and word help. If you pick French, German, Portuguese or Italian as your help language, it also downloads help in that language. These files come from our page on GitHub Pages (demirbasali.github.io), a free web hosting service run by GitHub.

The app only asks for the files. It does not send your name, your answers, your words or anything else you do in the app. Like any website, GitHub's servers see your phone's internet (IP) address when the files are downloaded, and may keep it in their logs for a short time for security. We don't receive or see this information. You can read GitHub's privacy statement.

If your phone is offline, the app still works with the texts it already has.

Read-aloud

When you tap Listen, your phone reads the text with its own built-in voice. The text is not sent anywhere.

"Save a copy"

In Settings you can tap Save a copy to share your practice, for example to email it to yourself. This only happens when you tap it, and you choose where it goes. We don't receive a copy.

Load a copy opens a file you pick yourself and puts that practice back on this phone.

Deleting your information

  • In the app: Settings → Start over removes your words, notes and reading from the phone.
  • Deleting the app from your phone also removes everything.

Children

Inklight is made for adults. It does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we ever change how the app handles information, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
