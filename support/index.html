Inklight help

Inklight is reading practice for adults learning English. Read real English texts, tap any word for help, and answer a few questions.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-in. Everything you do stays on your phone.

How do I get new texts?

New texts arrive by themselves. Open the app while your phone is online. They show up the next time you open the Library.

How do I get a better read-aloud voice?

Open your iPhone Settings and search for Voices. Choose English, then download a voice marked Premium or Enhanced. Then open Inklight, go to Settings → Read-aloud voice and pick the new voice.

How do I move my practice to a new phone?

  1. On your old phone, open Settings → Save a copy. Send the file to yourself, for example by email or to Files.
  2. On your new phone, install Inklight and open Settings → Load a copy. Pick the file.

How do I delete my information?

Open Settings → Start over. This removes your words, notes and reading from the phone. Deleting the app also removes everything.

How do I change my help language or level?

Open Settings. Tap Help language or Starting level.
